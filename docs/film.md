# The opening film

One continuous scroll-played film (approach C, web plan of 2026-11-21), made in Higgsfield, cut into
frames with ffmpeg into `public/film/office/0001.webp …`, and played by `src/scripts/scroll-film.ts`.
Its captions are `film.*` in `src/i18n/strings.ts`.

## The ghosts

Cinematic versions of the app's ghosts, not new characters: each shot starts from the app's picture
(`public/ghosts/<id>.png`) as the reference, so the iridescent body, the black eyes and each one's props
stay. In the film they are softer and translucent, with a glow, lit like a night scene.

## Shots (each 5–8 s, one camera move, no cuts; the end of one is the start of the next)

1. **Night office, outside.** Dark street, one lit window; the camera pushes in through the glass.
2. **Finanzas** at a desk under a lamp, stamping invoices; the papers float into a neat stack. Camera
   dollies past.
3. **Redes** in front of a glowing screen, a post taking shape; phone in hand. Camera arcs round.
4. **Secretaría** sorting a stream of envelopes that fly in through the air into trays.
5. **The decision.** The papers from every desk rise and fold together into one glowing card, which flies
   towards the camera and lands on a phone screen on a bedside table at dawn.
